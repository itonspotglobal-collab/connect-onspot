=== OnSpot Hiring & Talent Philosophy (from 4-Rules of Hiring Framework) ===

- OnSpot's talent philosophy revolves around identifying, hiring, and nurturing "A Players."
An A Player is defined as someone who:

- Gets It: Understands their role, business impact, and goals.

- Wants It: Is highly motivated and invested in growth.

- Has Capacity: Demonstrates the ability to perform at a high level.

- Lives the Core Values: Integrity, ownership, excellence, and empathy.

- Delivers Results: Consistently meets or exceeds expectations.

4 Rules of Hiring

- Hire for Will – Seek initiative and accountability.

- Match with Core Values – Non-negotiable cultural fit.

- Hire for Results – Proven history of measurable outcomes.

- Hire for Skill – Confirm capability and leadership potential.

- Recruitment uses:

- Job Success Profiles (JSPs)

- GWC (Get It, Want It, Capacity)

- Topgrading Interview Method

- A-Player Scorecards

=== OnSpot Talent Acquisition Framework ===

- The Talent Acquisition (TA) Process ensures hiring excellence via:

- Role Definition – Each job has a Job Success Profile.

- Sourcing & Screening – Talent is matched through the GWC and 4 Rules.

- Skills Assessment – Simulations and case studies to test real-world ability.

- Values Interview – Behavior-based questions for cultural alignment.

- Panel Evaluation – Consensus-based decision using structured scoring.

- OnSpot hires for potential and values as much as skill.

=== Job Applicant Guide ===

This section is for job seekers and candidates who want to find work through OnSpot.

--- How to Search for Jobs ---
Visit the OnSpot job board at /find-work/jobs to browse all open roles.
Use the search bar to find roles by keyword, job title, or skill.
Filter by: job category, location, work setup (Remote / Hybrid / On-site), and contract type.
Popular categories: Customer Support, Virtual Assistant, Bookkeeping, Sales, Operations, Marketing, Data Entry.
All listed jobs are managed by OnSpot and updated in real time.

--- How to Apply for a Job ---
Go to /find-work/jobs and browse available roles.
Click on a job card to open the full job detail page.
On the job detail page, click the "Apply" button to open the application form.
The form is quick — it asks for your basic information and is handled by OnSpot's recruitment team.
You only need to apply once; OnSpot will review your application and match you to suitable roles.

--- How to Create a Talent Profile (Get Matched Automatically) ---
For the best experience, create a full talent profile at /find-best-matches.
This is a 7-step process:
  Step 1 — Upload your Resume or CV (PDF or DOCX format).
  Step 2 — Review and edit your auto-extracted profile (name, contact, skills, work history, preferences).
  Step 3 — Create an account with your email and password, or log in if you already have one.
  Step 4 — Profile confirmed. You can continue to culture evaluation or view your profile.
  Step 5 — Complete the Culture Evaluation (values-based questions).
  Step 6 — See your Culture Alignment Score and breakdown.
  Step 7 — View your personalized Job Matches based on your profile and culture score.
The platform auto-extracts all information from your resume — no need to type everything manually.

--- How to Edit or Update Your Profile ---
Go to your public Talent Profile at /talent-profile/:id (your profile URL is shown after registration).
Click "Sign in to manage it" or the sign-in option, then enter your talent account email and password.
Once authenticated, all profile sections become inline-editable directly on the page.
You can edit: name, headline, about/summary, skills, work history, education, certifications, preferences, and portfolio links.

--- How to Upload or Update Your Resume ---
During registration: upload your PDF or DOCX resume in Step 1 of /find-best-matches.
After registration: go to your Talent Profile (/talent-profile/:id), sign in as the owner, and re-upload from the Resume section on the page.
Your resume is stored and can be downloaded by OnSpot recruiters.

--- How to Update Your Profile Photo ---
Go to your Talent Profile, sign in as the owner, then click the profile photo area to upload a new image.
Profile photos help recruiters and clients recognize you in the Talent Pool.

--- Application Status ---
OnSpot does not currently have a self-serve application status tracker in the portal.
For updates on your application, contact the OnSpot recruitment team: hr@onspotglobal.com
They handle all candidate-related inquiries and will respond promptly.

--- Interview Preparation Tips ---
OnSpot's hiring process includes: initial screening → skills/IQ/DISC assessment → behavioral (values) interview → panel evaluation.
To prepare:
  - Research OnSpot's 6 Core Values: People-Centric, Integrity, Beat Yesterday, Efficiency, Extreme Ownership, Intrapreneurial.
  - Prepare examples of measurable results you've achieved in past roles.
  - Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioral questions.
  - Be ready to discuss how you align with OnSpot's values — cultural fit is weighted equally with skills.
  - Demonstrate initiative, ownership, and a growth mindset.
OnSpot hires for Will, Values, Results, and Skill — in that order.
Skills gaps are coachable; behavior and value misalignment are disqualifying.

--- Resume Tips ---
Keep your resume clear, results-focused, and formatted as a PDF or DOCX.
Highlight measurable achievements (e.g. "Handled 80+ support tickets per day with 98% CSAT").
List specific tools and skills relevant to the role you are applying for.
Include a brief professional summary at the top.
The OnSpot resume parser reads PDF and DOCX formats — avoid image-only PDFs (scanned resumes may not parse correctly).
