SECTION: SERVICES OVERVIEW
================================================================================

MANAGED SERVICES
OnSpot Managed Services is a done-for-you execution engine — AI-first infrastructure, premium offshore talent, and active operational management in one integrated system.
Philosophy: AI First (systemize repetitive work first), Human Judgment (escalate what requires care), One System (one accountable execution layer).
Key Pillars: AI-First Delivery System, Managed Outcomes Not Just Hires, Humans When It Matters, Built for Operators.
Pricing:
- Lean Pod: 3 FTE + system — $6,000–$18,000/month (Early-stage SaaS, Support + Ops)
- Growth Pod: 5 FTE + system — $10,000–$30,000/month (Marketplace, CX + Driver Ops)
- Scale Pod: 8–10 FTE + system — $16,000–$50,000+/month (E-commerce, Full Back Office + CX)
How it works: (01) Diagnose the Work → (02) Build the System → (03) Deploy the Team → (04) Run and Improve
Vs Traditional Outsourcing: Traditional outsourcing sells headcount. OnSpot Managed Services delivers a managed operating system with AI, automation, governance, and talent in one motion.
FAQ: Pricing uses a per-FTE layer plus a platform and management layer based on workflow complexity.

RESOURCED SERVICES
Talent embedded directly into your team. Three models:
- Embedded Support: Talent that works inside your workflows, systems, and leadership rhythm. (Customer support, Executive support, Back-office ops, Admin coordination)
- Functional Pods: Small specialist teams built around a core function. (Recruiting, Sales support, Finance ops, QA and compliance)
- Scale Bench: Ready-to-ramp talent for launch, expansion, or seasonal spikes. (Fast onboarding, Hiring velocity, Shift coverage, Ramp planning)

ENTERPRISE SERVICES
Full-service outsourcing for enterprise clients with three-layer offerings: strategic, execution, and performance management.

HUMAN VIRTUAL ASSISTANT (HVA)
AI-powered human virtual assistant services with ROI calculator and pricing tiers. Features Framer Motion animations and capability tabs.

LEGALOPS
OnSpot LegalOps provides specialized legal operations support. Locations served: New York, Dallas, Houston, Austin, Miami, Orlando, Tampa, and more. Two models: Resourced Services and Managed Services for legal teams.

People First: Everything begins with people. We do not build systems at the expense of humanity — we build systems that elevate it. When our people feel seen, supported, and empowered, they do their best work. "People happiness drives client success."

Beat Yesterday: We are never finished. We chase progress daily — small improvements, better decisions, sharper execution. The standard is not perfection. The standard is growth. "Progress is our baseline."

Extreme Ownership: We do not pass problems. We own them. Every outcome, every challenge, every result is ours to solve. This is how we move fast and build trust. "No excuses. Only solutions."
