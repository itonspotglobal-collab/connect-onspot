---
name: Client invitation next step
description: Navigation and sequencing rule after Talent accepts a Client role invitation.
---

After Talent accepts a Client role invitation, keep them on My Applications and refresh invitations, applications, and interviews so the existing interview proposal becomes the immediate next step. Preserve the conversation thread and acceptance system message, but do not navigate there automatically.

**Why:** Role acceptance and interview scheduling are separate hiring actions; redirecting to Messages hid the required scheduling step.

**How to apply:** Changes to Client-invitation acceptance UX must preserve the backend transaction and messaging history while keeping the Talent on My Applications until they act on the associated interview.
