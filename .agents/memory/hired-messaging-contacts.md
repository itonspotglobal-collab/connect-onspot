---
name: Hired messaging contacts
description: Eligibility and privacy constraints for the hired-client and hired-talent contacts surface.
---

The hired-contacts surface must show only active hires with fully signed contracts, excluding void or terminated contracts. Do not relax eligibility to make test accounts appear.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requires active-hire-only contacts and truthful explanations when a test hire is ineligible.

**How to apply:** Apply the same eligibility to contact discovery and its new entry points. Preserve messaging-policy consent, PII review flags, name-reveal rules and canonical thread reuse. Do not confuse the hired-contacts scope with existing invitation/application messaging policies or silently rewrite those other workflows.

A scheduled future ending does not immediately end a hire; it remains contactable through its final work-timezone day. An administrative activation/waiver does not prove that signatures exist.

**Why:** The termination audit records when an ending was arranged, not necessarily when work ended. Treating the audit timestamp as immediate termination hides still-active hires; treating activation exceptions as signatures exposes unsigned engagements.

**How to apply:** Base ending eligibility on current contract state and its effective work-zone end date, and use actual signature/execution evidence for signed-contract eligibility.
