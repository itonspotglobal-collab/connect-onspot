---
name: Hired messaging contacts
description: Eligibility and privacy constraints for the hired-client and hired-talent contacts surface.
---

Hired messaging is independent of paperwork and billing eligibility: a formal-pipeline application marked hired can be messaged even with a draft or missing contract. Exclude invalid, void, terminated and ended hires.

**Why:** The owner explicitly permits urgent communication during pending paperwork, including audited Super Admin hires. Messaging must not imply financial activation or fabricated signatures.

**How to apply:** Apply messaging eligibility consistently to discovery, thread creation and sending. Reveal qualifying hired identities in messaging only, preserve other name-reveal policies, consent, PII flags and thread reuse, and show pending paperwork truthfully. Do not broaden billing, timesheet or roster eligibility.

A scheduled future ending does not immediately end a hire; it remains contactable through its final work-timezone day. An administrative activation/waiver does not prove that signatures exist.

**Why:** The termination audit records when an ending was arranged, not necessarily when work ended. Treating the audit timestamp as immediate termination hides still-active hires; treating activation exceptions as signatures exposes unsigned engagements.

**How to apply:** Base ending eligibility on current contract state and its effective work-zone end date. Use actual signatures/execution to describe paperwork completion, not to restrict hired messaging.
