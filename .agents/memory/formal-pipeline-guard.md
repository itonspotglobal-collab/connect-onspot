---
name: Hiring eligibility and privacy
description: Organic hiring is authorized without broadening invitation-only identity relationships.
---

In the normal Client/Talent flow, legitimate authenticated, linked Find Work applications are hireable on their original submission, alongside valid Client invitations. Do not require a duplicate Client invitation to hire an organic applicant. Silent shortlists, unlinked/imported records, mismatched owners and ineligible job/stage relationships remain excluded from that normal flow.

**Why:** The owner explicitly expanded hiring to organic applications; the former invitation-only hiring rule caused real interview/offer failures.

**How to apply:** Use common persisted ownership/identity/job eligibility for both paths, then preserve each action's role/stage validation and the signed-contract-only Hired transition.

## Owner-authorized legacy Admin hiring exception

Admin hiring overrides may operate on older applications irrespective of workflow type, initiator, registration status, job approval, draft status or scaffold origin. They still require a non-rejected/non-withdrawn/non-declined application, an existing linked user with role Talent, and an existing job owned by the application's Client. Never fabricate or repair an account link merely to enable an override.

**Why:** On 2026-11-03 the owner explicitly authorized legacy exceptions only through Admin hiring overrides; strict normal eligibility caused older applications to fail despite valid parties and ownership.

**How to apply:** Keep the shared normal hiring predicate and Client/Talent routes unchanged. Use a separate Admin-only eligibility rule. Downstream Admin preparation may recognize an unreverted override granted by a database-confirmed Admin, while rechecking the minimum account/ownership/status requirements. Keep audit reasons, truthful Waived/Bypassed states and explicit offer terms; missing job billing classification must link to the job editor, never infer defaults.

Name-reveal and invitation-established messaging remain separate, invitation-only relationships. Broader hiring eligibility is not permission to expose identities/contact details or open extra conversations.

**Why:** The hiring correction was scoped to interviews, offers and contracts, not a privacy or messaging redesign.

**How to apply:** Keep privacy relationship checks distinct when changing hiring predicates or adding new hiring endpoints.

The owner separately approved application-scoped messaging for job owners and their genuine direct applicants on 2026-10-21; see [Client↔talent messaging](client-talent-messaging.md).

**Why:** This is explicit messaging-policy approval, not an implied side effect of the broader hiring rules.

**How to apply:** Preserve invitation-only identity revelation and generic relationship creation while allowing the owned-application exception.

## Hiring UAT scope

Application milestones must reflect actual persisted transition history, not assumed completion of every stage preceding the current status.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requires that the Talent timeline never advance visually without the corresponding backend event; legitimate workflows may skip stages.

**How to apply:** Render recorded events and keep the live canonical status separate. An overdue offer can be annotated without fabricating an application-status transition.

The owner states that the normal production hiring workflow must not be changed merely to support testing. Do not invent interviews/signatures or weaken normal formal hiring guards. Manual Hired is allowed only through the separately owner-authorized, audited Admin overrides described above. Prefer normal Admin preparation instructions over a test-only utility when existing actions suffice.

**Why:** UAT is intended to exercise real downstream Offer, Contract, signature-driven Hired, Team and Timesheet behavior, not substitute a testing pipeline for it.

**How to apply:** Audit existing preparation actions first. Add a separate fail-closed, explicitly test-account-restricted utility only if normal actions cannot prepare legitimate test records. Preserve all downstream activation and ownership checks.
