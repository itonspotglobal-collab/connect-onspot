---
name: Hiring eligibility and privacy
description: Organic hiring is authorized without broadening invitation-only identity relationships.
---

Legitimate authenticated, linked Find Work applications are hireable on their original submission, alongside valid Client invitations. Do not require a duplicate Client invitation to hire an organic applicant. Silent shortlists, unlinked/imported records, mismatched owners and ineligible job/stage relationships remain excluded.

**Why:** The owner explicitly expanded hiring to organic applications; the former invitation-only hiring rule caused real interview/offer failures.

**How to apply:** Use common persisted ownership/identity/job eligibility for both paths, then preserve each action's role/stage validation and the signed-contract-only Hired transition.

Name-reveal and invitation-established messaging remain separate, invitation-only relationships. Broader hiring eligibility is not permission to expose identities/contact details or open extra conversations.

**Why:** The hiring correction was scoped to interviews, offers and contracts, not a privacy or messaging redesign.

**How to apply:** Keep privacy relationship checks distinct when changing hiring predicates or adding new hiring endpoints.
