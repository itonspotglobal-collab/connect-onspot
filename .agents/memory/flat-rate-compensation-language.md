---
name: Flat-rate compensation language
description: Product-language constraint for OnSpot's engagement and compensation model.
---

Public product copy, sample dashboards, and illustrative rates must not use hourly language or `/hr` amounts. Use flat period-based terms such as a monthly rate instead.

**Why:** OnSpot supports Half-Day and Full-Time engagement types with flat rates per period, not an hourly billing model. This rule aligns the public experience with the enforced product data model.

**How to apply:** Audit marketing pages, hero mockups, dashboards, and other user-visible sample content for hourly wording whenever compensation copy is created or changed.
