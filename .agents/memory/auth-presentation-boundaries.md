Keep the established white rounded signup portal and its role cards, colors, icons, typography, and copy. The Client and Talent signup forms retain their navy design. Moving signup into a modal is not authorization to redesign the chooser.

**Why:** The owner rejected replacing the white portal with navy role-selection cards when asking for an overlay on the actual originating page.

**How to apply:** Reuse the established portal presentation inside the modal; change the surrounding page/overlay behavior rather than inventing a second chooser design.

Display the exact official white logo artwork with genuine alpha transparency. Never add a rectangular navy background plate around the logo or redraw the wordmark as text. A restrained edge/shadow for contrast on light surfaces is allowed; preserve proportions and original artwork colors.

**Why:** The owner explicitly rejected the dark logo rectangle introduced as a contrast treatment.

**How to apply:** Verify transparent pixels in the actual image, not just its file extension, and inspect both light and dark auth surfaces. Do not mask an opaque image background with CSS.
