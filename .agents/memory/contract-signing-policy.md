---
name: Contract signing parties
description: Required signatures for uploaded OnSpot, Client and Organization agreements.
---

OnSpot must always sign before Hired/billing activation. OnSpot agreements require Talent and OnSpot. Client/Organization agreements additionally require the authenticated authorized Client/Organization representative, attributed to a real person.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose to retain OnSpot's required signature when adding Client/Organization signing, rather than replace the existing intermediary contract approval.

**How to apply:** Treat Client/Organization acceptance as an additional requirement, never as an OnSpot signature. Bind each signature to the reviewed immutable PDF and reuse canonical contract activation only after all required signatures.

Unsigned legacy timestamp-only contracts must be voided/reissued through the PDF workflow, not silently adopted with their existing timestamps. Preserve fully signed historical rows without claiming they have new version-bound signature evidence.

**Why:** The older acceptance timestamps do not prove which document bytes were reviewed. Rebinding them to a newly uploaded PDF would manufacture signing evidence.

**How to apply:** Any future legacy migration must collect fresh consent for a new PDF version rather than transfer prior timestamps.
