Current issue:
The Insights section currently looks like another strip/card row directly below the stats section, which creates a “double strip” effect:
1. Stats strip
2. Insights strip

Goal:
Update the Insights section into a minimal, clean Apple Newsroom-inspired layout:
- More editorial
- More spacious but not too tall
- Not a strip
- Not visually competing with the Stats section
- Still compact enough for the homepage

IMPORTANT:
- Do not copy Apple’s design exactly.
- Use Apple Newsroom only as inspiration for minimal layout, large image treatment, clean spacing, and editorial hierarchy.
- Keep OnSpot branding, fonts, colors, and article content.
- Keep the section on the homepage.
- Make it responsive.
