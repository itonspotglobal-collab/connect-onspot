FINAL, complete model for Talent invoicing — this SUPERSEDES the earlier
"Talent submits a claim, Admin reviews for discrepancy against
claimed/computed/approved amounts" design. Do not build that version;
build this one.

ARCHITECTURE:
1. TIMESHEET is the single source of truth for attendance/hours. The
   Talent can dispute/edit their timesheet; edits require approval
   (audit first: does a real timesheet/attendance data source and edit-
   approval workflow already exist today — e.g. the legacy Attendance
   Dashboard mentioned in earlier session history — or does this need to
   be built from scratch? Report before proposing further).
2. INVOICE is a pure, mechanical, auto-generated derivation from the
   currently-approved timesheet for that period. The Talent NEVER edits
   an invoice directly and never independently claims a number - there
   is no "claimed vs platform-computed" divergence at the invoice level
   at all, since the invoice IS the timesheet's numbers. Drop the
   claimed_amount/platform_computed_amount/discrepancy-review fields
   from the earlier schema proposal entirely.
3. At the end of each calendar-aligned period (1st-15th, 16th-end of
   month), the system automatically generates an invoice DRAFT from the
   approved timesheet and notifies the Talent it's ready for review.
4. The Talent reviews the draft. If they disagree, they go edit the
   TIMESHEET (a separate action/page), which triggers the approval
   workflow from point 1; once that edit is approved, the invoice draft
   is regenerated to reflect it.
5. If the Talent agrees, they click Send. Once sent, the invoice goes to
   the OnSpot ledger for batch processing toward the fixed payment dates
   (25th for the 1st-15th period, 5th of the next month for the 16th-end
   period).
6. DEADLINE: 48 hours from when the draft/notification is sent. If the
   Talent does not respond within 48 hours, the invoice AUTO-SENDS as-is
   (implicit agreement). This fires regardless of any pending, unresolved
   timesheet dispute - do NOT pause or block auto-send for a pending
   dispute.
7. CREDIT MEMO mechanism: if a timesheet correction is approved AFTER
   its invoice has already auto-sent (or been sent), do NOT reopen or
   reprice that already-sent invoice. Instead, create a credit_memo
   record capturing the delta (positive or negative), linked to the
   original period/invoice and the approving timesheet edit, and apply
   it as a line-item adjustment on the NEXT period's invoice draft when
   it's generated. Document this reasoning clearly in code comments -
   this is the same non-retroactive principle already used for the
   Loyalty tier and commission-rate versioning elsewhere in this build.
8. TIMEZONE: all deadline/cutoff computation is anchored to US Eastern
   Time (matching OnSpot's Delaware entity), never a Talent's local
   time. This is the single canonical clock for global consistency as
   the Talent pool expands beyond the Philippines. When DISPLAYING a
   deadline or payment date to a Talent, convert it to their local time
   for clarity - the underlying cutoff computation must never change
   based on Talent location.

Client-side monthly invoice generation, the legacy invoice_periods
preservation, and the deposit-coverage check (never treating a pending
deposit as collected) from the prior rounds all still stand unchanged.
