2. Email sender

All talent/application emails must be sent from:

careers@onspotglobal.com

The applicant's email is the recipient, never the sender.

Backend email configuration should follow this pattern:

From: OnSpot Careers <careers@onspotglobal.com>
To: applicant email
Reply-To: careers@onspotglobal.com

Do not trust a from address supplied by the frontend. Set the sender on the server.

Put the sender address in configuration/environment variables where appropriate, for example:

EMAIL_FROM=OnSpot Careers <careers@onspotglobal.com>
EMAIL_REPLY_TO=careers@onspotglobal.com

Use the project's existing email provider/service. Verify that careers@onspotglobal.com or the onspotglobal.com domain is authorized/verified with that provider.
