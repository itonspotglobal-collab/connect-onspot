## 6. Preserve the manual payment verification workflow

The existing payment process must remain:

1. Client views the amount due and Wise QR code.
2. Client pays externally.
3. Client uploads payment receipt and enters the transaction reference.
4. Payment status becomes **Pending Verification**.
5. Super Admin reviews the submitted receipt.
6. Super Admin independently confirms the actual Wise settlement.
7. Payment is marked **Paid** only after verification.

If verification fails:

- Admin can reject the submission.
- Admin must provide a rejection reason.
- Client can resubmit proof.
- Previous submissions remain in the audit history.

**Displaying or scanning the QR code must never automatically mark a payment as paid.**
