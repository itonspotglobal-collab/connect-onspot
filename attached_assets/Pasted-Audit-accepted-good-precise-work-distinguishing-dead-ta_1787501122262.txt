CORRECTED escalation ladder for security_deposits, replacing what was
proposed:

1. Invoice unpaid → deposit draws against to keep Talent paid (unchanged
   mechanism).
2. Day 5 → Client must replenish the deposit to its required level.
3. Day 15, still unresolved → SUSPENSION triggers (services pause) plus
   a further cure period begins (exact length still being confirmed —
   do not hardcode a number yet, make it a configurable value).
4. If Client cures during that window → suspension lifts, deposit stays
   intact, engagement continues normally.
5. If Client does NOT cure → engagement terminates SPECIFICALLY for
   non-payment, and the deposit is FORFEITED IN FULL.

CRITICAL DISTINCTION — forfeiture applies ONLY when termination happens
because of this non-payment path. A Client terminating for any other
reason (normal notice, mutual end of engagement, no missed payments)
still gets the deposit applied to their final invoice as before — this
is NOT a blanket "any early termination forfeits the deposit" rule.

Schema changes needed:
- Add 'suspended' and 'forfeited' to the security_deposits status enum
  (currently: pending | held | drawn | replenishment_pending | applied |
  void — missing both of these).
- Add a field distinguishing WHY a deposit reached a terminal state (e.g.
  termination_reason or forfeiture_reason) so 'applied' (normal end) and
  'forfeited' (non-payment breach) are unambiguous in the data, not just
  inferred from status.
- Add suspended_at and a cure-deadline field (name TBD) tracking the
  Day-15-triggered cure window separately from the Day-5 replenishment
  deadline — these are two distinct clocks, not the same one.

Do NOT proceed with Phase 1 until this is incorporated — the exact
