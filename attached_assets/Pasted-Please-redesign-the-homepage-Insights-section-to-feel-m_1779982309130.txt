Current issue:
The Insights section currently feels like a standard blog/news card grid. We do not want readers to immediately leave the homepage for the Insights page. We want this section to act as a visual teaser that adds credibility and interest while keeping the homepage flow intact.

Goal:
Make the Insights section more visual, more immersive, and less like a traditional blog archive.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT change the Navigation bar.
- Do NOT change the Footer.
- Do NOT change the Hero section.
- Do NOT change other homepage sections.
- Do NOT remove the Insights section.
- Do NOT use the exact Mindvalley design, only use it as inspiration.

- Keep real published Insights/blog content and images.
- Do not hardcode fake articles if real insight data exists.
- Keep the “Explore all insights →” link, but make it secondary/subtle.

==================================================
DESIGN DIRECTION
==================================================

Change the Insights section from a standard 3-card blog grid into a more visual “featured insight gallery” or “visual knowledge strip.”

- premium

- homepage-friendly
- easy to scan
- not too text-heavy

The user should be able to get value from the section without immediately clicking away.
