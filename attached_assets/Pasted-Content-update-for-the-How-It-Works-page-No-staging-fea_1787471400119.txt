**Q: How do I get Verified?**
"Verified" confirms your identity and credentials — you'll upload a

**Q: How do I get Vetted?**
"Vetted" goes further than Verified — it confirms your skills,
experience, and work history are accurate, through OnSpot's internal
review. You must be Verified first. From there, there's more than one
path:
- **Request it** — reach out to your account manager and ask for a
  review.
- **Get selected** — OnSpot may proactively offer it to Contractors with
  strong performance.
- **Reach a milestone** — it can also be granted automatically once you
  hit a tenure or engagement threshold.

INSERT this new question right after "How do I get Vetted?":

**Q: What's the difference between Verified and Vetted?**
Verified confirms *who you are* — your identity and certifications are
on file and reviewed. Vetted confirms *how you perform* — your skills,
experience, and work history have been reviewed by OnSpot. Verified is
the foundation; Vetted is the deeper layer built on top of it.

**Q: Are Contractors verified or vetted before I can hire them?**
Not every Contractor carries these marks — both are optional, not
automatic.
- **Verified** means OnSpot has confirmed their identity and
  certifications.
- **Vetted** means OnSpot has gone further, reviewing their skills,
  experience, and work history — and requires that they're already
  Verified first.

Look for either mark on a profile if trust signals matter for your
hiring decision.
