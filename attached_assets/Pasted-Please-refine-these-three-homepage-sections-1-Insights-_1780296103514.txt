3. SUPERHUMAN NETWORK — FIX CROPPED FACES / IMAGE POSITIONING
==================================================

Current issue:
Some photos in the Superhuman Network image mosaic are cropped badly. In particular, individual profile photos have faces cut off or not shown properly because of the image size/crop.

Goal:
Adjust image cropping so important faces are visible, especially individual people photos.

Requirements:
- Keep the mosaic layout.
- Do not replace the whole section design.
- Adjust image fit and object-position per image where needed.
- Prioritize showing faces clearly.
- Avoid cutting off heads/chins in individual portraits.
- Keep the images filling their grid cells cleanly.

General image style:
className="
  h-full w-full object-cover
"

But add specific object-position values for problematic photos.

For individual/person images:
Use one of these depending on the image:
- object-center
- object-top
- object-[center_25%]
- object-[center_30%]
- object-[center_35%]

Example:
<img
  src="..."
  className="h-full w-full object-cover object-[center_25%]"
/>

For the photo where the person’s face is cropped:
- Move crop upward if the face is too low:
  object-[center_25%]
- Move crop downward if forehead/top is cut:
  object-[center_40%]
- If full face needs to show and cropping still fails, use object-contain with a dark background only for that specific image:
  className="h-full w-full object-contain bg-slate-900"

Preferred:
Use object-cover with better object-position first.

For portrait/individual photo cells:
If the cell is too short to show faces properly, slightly increase the row height or specific cell height.

Example mosaic container:
className="
  grid auto-rows-[180px] md:auto-rows-[220px] lg:auto-rows-[260px]
"

For individual portrait cells:
className="min-h-[260px]"

Do not make all images object-contain because the mosaic may look inconsistent. Only use it where cropping is impossible to fix.

Expected result:
The Superhuman Network photo mosaic should still look dynamic, but individual faces should be visible and not awkwardly cropped. The “Focused Expertise” card/photo should show the person’s face properly.
