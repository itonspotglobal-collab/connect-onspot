Refine the hero layout on the public Job Details page so the Additional Compensation section is more compact.

Primary file:
- `client/src/pages/FindWorkJob.tsx`

CURRENT ISSUE

The current hero layout renders:

Benefits | Additional Compensation

as two equal-width cards.

Because Additional Compensation may only contain:
- Commission
- Equity

the card becomes unnecessarily wide and looks visually empty.

GOAL

Make Additional Compensation size itself more naturally to its content while keeping Benefits visually dominant.

1. CHANGE THE BENEFITS / ADDITIONAL COMPENSATION LAYOUT

Instead of:

```tsx
grid sm:grid-cols-2

use a layout where Benefits takes the remaining width and Additional Compensation is content-sized.

Preferred desktop structure:

<div className="flex flex-col gap-3 lg:flex-row lg:items-stretch">
  <div className="min-w-0 flex-1">
    {/* Benefits */}
  </div>

  <div className="lg:w-auto lg:min-w-fit">
    {/* Additional Compensation */}
  </div>
</div>
BENEFITS CARD

Benefits should take most of the available width because it can contain multiple items.

Example:

<div className="
  flex-1
  rounded-xl
  border border-white/10
  bg-white/5
  px-4 py-3
">

<div className="

  px-4 py-3

Additional Compensation
