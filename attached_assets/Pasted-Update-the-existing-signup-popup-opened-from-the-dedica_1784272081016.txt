Scrolling and viewport fix:

8. Make the dialog usable at every screen height.
- The overlay should cover the viewport.
- The dialog content must never extend beyond the reachable viewport.
- Add a maximum height based on the viewport, for example:
  `max-h-[calc(100dvh-2rem)]`
- Use `overflow-hidden` on the outer dialog container.
- Create an internal scrollable content area using:
  `overflow-y-auto`
- Keep the header and close button visible when practical.
- The full form, terms checkbox, submit button, and footer links must all be reachable by scrolling.
- Scrolling should happen inside the modal, not leave the page stuck.
- Use `overscroll-contain` where useful.
- Support modern mobile viewport units such as `dvh`.

Recommended structure:

<DialogContent className="... max-h-[calc(100dvh-2rem)] overflow-hidden ...">
  <div className="flex max-h-[calc(100dvh-2rem)] flex-col">
    <div className="shrink-0">
      modal header
    </div>

    <div className="min-h-0 flex-1 overflow-y-auto overscroll-contain">
      signup step content
    </div>
  </div>
</DialogContent>
