THE DESIGN TO BUILD
═══════════════════════════════════════

PART 1 — Combined Invite + Interview
Client sends ONE Invite that includes both the job selection and
proposed interview time(s) — not a bare invite followed by separate
scheduling later. Talent's response is three-way:
- Accept a proposed time
- Decline entirely (ends the pipeline)
- Counter-propose different times

Mechanics: an interviews table tracks proposed times and whose turn it is
to respond (proposed_by: 'client' | 'talent'). Do NOT increment a round
counter for this negotiation back-and-forth — that's reserved for
genuinely separate interview stages (e.g. a technical round after this
one). No hard cap on rounds, but add a soft nudge after ~3 rounds
suggesting one side pick from the other's times.
