Design a single responsive section for a landing page with a Replit-style dark SaaS vibe.

Goals:
- The entire section must fit in one laptop viewport (max height ~900–1000px).
- Layout: one clear row with two main elements: a US map on the left and a regions panel on the right.
- The design should feel seamless, clean, simple, powerful, and “alive” (subtle motion and glow), not corporate or boxy.

Structure:
1. Section background:
   - Full-width dark theme.
   - Use a radial or subtle linear gradient (deep navy to dark gray, e.g. #020617 to #111827).
   - Enough contrast so cards sit on top and feel elevated.

2. Header (top of section):
   - Small eyebrow label: “ONSPOT COVERAGE” in all caps, tiny tracking, muted blue/gray.
   - Main title: “We’re Across America” in large, bold, light text.
   - Short subtitle below in softer gray-blue. Max width ~520px.
   - Header should sit above the grid, left-aligned.

3. Main grid:
   - Two columns on desktop: 
     - Left: map card (about 55–60% width).
     - Right: regions panel card (40–45%).
   - On mobile, stack map on top, regions panel below, full width.

4. Map card (left):
   - Dark, semi-transparent card (#0f172a with alpha).
   - Large border-radius (20–24px), soft shadow, no hard borders.
   - Inside, place a simplified gray US map image with OnSpot-blue pins.
   - Add a subtle inner blue glow behind the center of the map using a radial gradient.
   - At the bottom-left of the card, add a small pill-shaped label with:
     - A tiny green status dot with a glow.
     - Text like “Live legal ops pods across the U.S.”
   - Everything should feel like a Replit/Stripe dashboard widget, not a plain image block.

5. Regions panel (right):
   - Another dark card matching the map card radius and shadow.
   - Title/label at the top: “Regions we’re active in” in small uppercase.
   - Below, a vertical list of 4 regions:
       - Tri-State — NY • NJ • CT · High-volume practices
       - Texas — Dallas • Houston • Austin · Growing firms
       - Florida — Miami • Orlando • Tampa · Seasonal expertise
       - California — Bay Area • LA · Tech-focused firms
   - Each region row:
       - No full-width boxes; use pill-like hover states only.
       - Layout: small colored dot on the left, then region name (bold) and description (muted).
       - On hover: slight lift (translateY(-1px)), subtle brighter background (dark blue), and soft shadow.

6. Footer pill inside the regions panel:
   - Small dashed-outline pill at the bottom with:
       - A small cyan or blue glowing dot.
       - Text like: “Ready to open another region? Plug in a new pod in weeks.”
   - Keep it subtle and secondary.

7. Typography and colors:
   - Use a modern sans-serif (system UI or something like Inter).
   - Main text: light gray (#e5e7eb), muted text: #9ca3af.
   - Accent blue: use a bright but not neon blue (e.g. #3b82f6) for dots, links, and pins.
   - Do NOT use bright white backgrounds; everything should be dark-mode, soft, and consistent.

8. Interactions:
   - Smooth transitions (150–200ms) on hover for region rows.
   - No crazy animations; just tiny motion and opacity changes to feel alive.

9. Constraints:
   - Avoid multiple nested boxes; only 2 main cards (map and regions) in this section.
   - Ensure the section looks good on MacBook 13–15" without vertical scrolling.
   - Make it responsive and accessible (good contrast, readable font sizes).

Implement this in HTML/CSS (or React + Tailwind) with a focus on visual polish and clean layout.
