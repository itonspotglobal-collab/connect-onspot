Please make a final adjustment to the Work Differently section heading.

Current issue:
The heading is almost correct, but it is still too wide and gets cut off horizontally on the right side. We want the heading to fit cleanly inside the viewport.

Desired layout on desktop/laptop:

Line 1:
Whether you're scaling a team or growing a career

Line 2:
— OnSpot is built for both sides of great work.

Goal:
Keep the title large and prominent, but reduce the size just enough so both lines fit cleanly without being cut off.

IMPORTANT:
- Do not make the title small.
- Do not allow horizontal overflow.
- Do not let the text get clipped at the sides.
- Keep it centered.
- Keep the same font family and bold weight.
- Keep the two-line structure.
- Let it wrap naturally on tablet/mobile.

Recommended fix:

1. Use a slightly smaller fluid font size
Replace the current oversized heading size with:

className="
  mx-auto max-w-[1500px]
  px-6 sm:px-8 lg:px-10
  text-center font-bold tracking-tight text-slate-950
  leading-[1.08]
  text-[clamp(34px,3.55vw,58px)]
"

This should still look like a major section title, but not overflow.

2. Keep each line on one line only for larger screens
Use:

<span className="block xl:whitespace-nowrap">
  Whether you're scaling a team or growing a career
</span>

<span className="mt-2 block xl:whitespace-nowrap">
  — OnSpot is built for both sides of great work.
</span>

Important:
- Use `xl:whitespace-nowrap`, not `lg:whitespace-nowrap`, so smaller laptop screens can wrap if needed.
- Do not use `whitespace-nowrap` globally because it causes overflow.

3. Add container padding
Make sure the heading container has side padding:

px-6 sm:px-8 lg:px-10

This prevents the text from touching or clipping against the viewport edge.

4. Prevent section overflow
Make sure the section wrapper does not allow horizontal overflow:

className="
  relative overflow-hidden
"

or at minimum:
overflow-x-hidden

5. Mobile/tablet behavior
For tablet and mobile:
- The text can wrap naturally.
- It does not need to stay exactly two lines.
- It should never overflow horizontally.

Expected result:
On large desktop screens, the heading appears as two clean lines:

Whether you're scaling a team or growing a career
— OnSpot is built for both sides of great work.

On smaller screens, it wraps naturally without being cut off.
The title remains bold, large, and visually important.
