Fix Talent accounts being incorrectly created or identified as Client accounts.

CURRENT ISSUE

I created a new account through Sign Up and selected Talent.

When I try to log in with the correct email and password and select Talent Portal, the app says:

“This is a Client account. Please use the Client Portal.”

Password reset succeeds, but login still gives the same Client-account message.

This proves the issue is not the password anymore.

The account role is either:

- being saved as `client` during Talent signup,
- being read incorrectly during login,
- defaulting to `client` when the role is missing,
- or the email is linked to conflicting Client and Talent records.

GOAL

A user who selects Talent during signup must be saved and authenticated as:

```text
role = talent

After signup or login, the user must be redirected to their own Talent Profile page.

Do not change the Client flow.
Do not remove portal-role validation.
Do not solve this by ignoring role mismatches.
