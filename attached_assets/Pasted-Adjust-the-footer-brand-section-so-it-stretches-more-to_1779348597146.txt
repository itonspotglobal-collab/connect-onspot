Adjust the footer brand section so it stretches more toward the left side and is aligned with the bottom copyright text.

Goal:
Move/stretch the brand section toward the left side while keeping the Navigation column separated and clean.

Requirements:

1. Align brand section with copyright text
- The left edge of the brand/logo section should align with:
  “© 2025 OnSpot. All rights reserved.”
- The OnSpot logo, brand description, and social icons should all follow this same left alignment.
- Do not center the brand column if it causes it to move inward.

- Keep the brand column wider than the other footer columns.
- Increase the gap between the brand section and the Navigation column.
- The Navigation column should not feel too close to the brand description or social icons.

Suggested grid update:
Use a wider first column and larger desktop column gap.

className="container mx-auto px-6 py-10 sm:py-12"

- text-center
- items-center
- justify-center

className="mx-0"
