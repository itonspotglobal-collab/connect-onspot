A letter from Nur Laminero

Co-Founder and CEO, OnSpot

We built a system for small and medium businesses to solve the broken choice between freelance chaos and enterprise BPO overhead.

The problem

Today, businesses that need operational support have to either manage unreliable freelancers on their own or pay enterprise prices for BPO services that were never designed for them. Meanwhile talented professionals around the world spend their time searching for clients instead of doing the work they are great at — and earn a fraction of what they are worth.

Our solution

With OnSpot, businesses find, hire, and manage vetted global talent in as fast as 7 days — with the accountability of a managed service and none of the overhead — while talent sets their own rate, keeps every dollar, and builds a real international career.

My co-founders Jake and Mark and I spent four years running a BPO before building this platform. We did not guess at the problem. We lived inside it — and we built OnSpot from operational truth rather than theory. We are already self-sustaining. Operations are fully covered by revenue before a single dollar of external investment.

We are raising to launch, to scale, and to liberate how the world does meaningful work. A world where anyone can work anywhere — and every company can hire anyone, without limits.

NL
Nur LamineroCo-Founder and CEO, OnSpot
