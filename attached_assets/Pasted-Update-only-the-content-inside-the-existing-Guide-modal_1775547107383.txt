1.0 PURPOSE
This document outlines the Talent Acquisition Topgrading Process of OnSpot in alignment with ISO 9001:2015 standards. The objective is to ensure the consistent recruitment of A-Players who exhibit the following:
- Strong Will and Motivation
- Alignment with OnSpot Core Values
- Proven Ability to Deliver Results
- Skills and Competencies for Role Success



2.0 SCOPE
This procedure applies to all internal and external hiring for regular, project-based, and contractual roles at OnSpot.



3.0 DEFINITIONS
- A-Player – Top 10% of talent available for any given role.
- Job Success Profile (JSP) – A framework defining success attributes and outcomes required for a role.
- Topgrading – A hiring methodology aimed at systematically identifying high performers through deep-dive screening.



4.0 RESPONSIBILITIES
- Head of People and Workplace – Oversees implementation of the process.
- Talent Acquisition Manager – Executes the process and ensures ISO compliance.
- Hiring Managers – Participate in interviews and final decisions.
- QMR – Audits process alignment with ISO 9001:2015.



5.0 PROCEDURE



STAGE 1: PREPARATION & JOB SCORECARD
- Create Job Success Profile (JSP) based on:
  - Key Accountabilities (3–5 measurable outcomes)
  - Core Values and Behaviors
  - Technical and Soft Skills
- Develop an A-Player Scorecard including:
  - Core Competencies
  - Performance Metrics
  - Culture Fit Indicators
- Use Standard Template: A-Player Job Scorecard



STAGE 2: ATTRACTING A-PLAYERS
- Publish Smart Job Ads using compelling, results-focused language.
- Emphasize OnSpot's culture, growth opportunity, and impact.
- Include self-screening challenge questions.
- Post across Careers Page, LinkedIn, and relevant platforms.



STAGE 3: WRITTEN PRE-SCREENING
- Collect initial applications through ATS.
- Include required written responses:
  - Compensation History and Expectation
  - Motivation for Role
  - Career Goals
  - Manager Assessment Insights
  - Core Values Alignment Scenario (Mandatory)
- Disqualify applicants who skip value-alignment question.
- Invite qualified candidates to complete the Career History Form (CHF).



STAGE 4: CAREER HISTORY FORM (CHF)
- Candidate submits CHF with:
  - Full Salary History
  - Past Manager Names and Contacts
  - Key Achievements and Failures
  - Self and Manager Performance Ratings
  - Reasons for Leaving Roles
- Notify candidate about reference checks to ensure transparency.



STAGE 5: INITIAL SCREENING INTERVIEW
- Conduct interview using the "Gets It, Wants It, Capacity to Do It" model.
- Key Evaluation Areas:
  - Role Understanding and Motivation
  - Personal and Professional Capacity
  - Environmental and Cultural Alignment
- Document findings using Interview Notes Sheet.



STAGE 6: TOPGRADING INTERVIEW (DEEP DIVE)
- Hiring Manager conducts a chronological interview covering all roles since college.
- Capture information on:
  - Role Objectives
  - Successes and Failures
  - Team and Manager Feedback
  - Departure Reason
- Use structured follow-up for pattern recognition.
- One interviewer leads; one (or AI Agent) records detailed notes.



STAGE 7: COMPETENCY INTERVIEWS (MANAGER LEVEL ROLES)
- Conduct a panel or functional interview.
- Assess top 3–5 competencies from the scorecard.
- Include scenario-based and behavioral questions.
- Client Interview required for Delivery-related positions.



STAGE 8: TORC REFERENCE CHECKING
- Require candidate to facilitate 2–3 reference calls.
- Suggested Questions:
  - Relationship and Tenure
  - Performance, Integrity, Team Fit
  - Rehire Likelihood
- Use TORC to validate claims and eliminate dishonest candidates.



STAGE 9: HIRING DECISION
- Use the Topgrading Decision Grid:
  - Performance Record
  - Competency Match
  - Values Alignment
  - Coachability
  - Compensation Fit
- Hire only if all criteria are met with evidence.
- Disqualify candidates with consistent blame behavior or lack of evidence.



STAGE 10: POST-HIRE COACHING
- Share the Job Scorecard on Day 1.
- Provide a 30-60-90 Day Plan.
- Hold monthly check-ins on outcomes.
- Review Topgrading interview inputs and build coaching alignment.



6.0 DOCUMENTED INFORMATION (TEMPLATES REQUIRED)
- Job Success Profile Template
- A-Player Job Scorecard Template
- Career History Form (CHF)
- TORC Reference Script
- Interview Notes Sheet
- Topgrading Decision Grid



7.0 QUALITY ASSURANCE AND AUDIT
- Regular process audits conducted by QMR.
- Feedback loops established from Hiring Managers and New Hires.
- Annual process review and template updates.



8.0 RECORDS RETENTION
All candidate documents shall be retained for 2 years in secure HRIS or ATS.
