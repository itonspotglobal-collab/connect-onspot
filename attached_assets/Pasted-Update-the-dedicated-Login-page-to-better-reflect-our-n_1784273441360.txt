Update the dedicated Login page to better reflect our new OnSpot brand direction.

This is a visual/content update only.
Do NOT modify the authentication logic, API calls, validation, portal selection, or routing.

## 1. Update branding

Replace every occurrence of:

"GLOBAL OUTSOURCING PLATFORM"

with

"WORK WITHOUT LIMITS"

Use the exact capitalization shown above.

This applies to the dedicated login page only.

---

## 2. Lighten the overall visual style

Our new business direction is moving toward a lighter, cleaner premium look while still keeping the existing purple/blue OnSpot identity.

Do NOT redesign the page.
Keep the same layout and spacing.

Instead, adjust the color palette:

### Background
Keep the existing dark gradient/grid background, but make it slightly brighter and less saturated.

### Login Card
Make the card lighter.

Current:
- very dark navy

Target:
- rich indigo / deep violet
- slightly brighter than today
- premium SaaS appearance

Example direction:

background:
rgba(56, 54, 124, 0.9)

instead of nearly black.

---

## 3. Typography

Increase contrast.

Use:

Headings:
- white

Body text:
- light lavender
- slate-200 style

Labels:
- almost white

Placeholder text:
- muted lavender

Avoid gray text that becomes difficult to read.

---

## 4. Links

Make all links noticeably lighter.

This includes:

- Forgot password?
- Create Account
- Back to Home

Current:
Muted gray.

Target:
Light lavender / soft white.

Hover:
Bright white.

Example Tailwind direction:

text-indigo-200
hover:text-white

or

text-violet-200
hover:text-white

The links should be immediately visible without looking overly bright.

Keep the Client and Talent cards exactly as they are structurally.
