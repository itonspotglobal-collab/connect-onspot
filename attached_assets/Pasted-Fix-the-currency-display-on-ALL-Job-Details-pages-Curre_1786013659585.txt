Fix the currency display on ALL Job Details pages.

Current problem:
The Monthly Compensation card has a hard-coded "$" icon while the actual
currency can be USD, PHP, or another supported currency.

Example of the current bad UI:

$   Monthly Compensation (USD)
    USD 3,100 – 3,300/month

This is redundant, and PHP jobs incorrectly still show "$".

CHANGE IT TO:

Monthly Compensation
USD 3,100 – 3,300/month

or:

Monthly Compensation
PHP 50,000 – 60,000/month

   BAD:

   USD 3,100 – 3,300/month
   PHP 50,000 – 60,000/month

   Never hard-code USD, PHP, "$", or "₱".

5. Keep our standardized monthly compensation format:
   [CURRENCY] [AMOUNT]/month

6. For ranges:
   USD 3,100 – 3,300/month

7. For single values:
   PHP 50,000/month

8. Apply this consistently across ALL connected job UI:
   - Job Details
   - Find Work / All Jobs
   - job cards
   - Featured Job cards
   - job preview
   - admin public-page preview
   - any other component displaying compensation

   "$"
   "USD"
   "₱"
   "/year"
   "/month"

   Do not blindly replace them. Determine whether each occurrence is
   related to job compensation and update the shared formatter/component.

    consistently.

Example:

  min: 3100,

=> "USD 3,100 – 3,300/month"
