Before any timesheet/invoice-automation work begins: the timesheet needs
a real Time In / Time Out mechanism as its actual source of truth, not a
retroactive manual-hours-entry form. "Talent submits daily entries" means
the Talent clocks in and clocks out in real time from within the
platform; the system derives daily/period hours from those captured
events, which then feed the timesheet review/approval/dispute workflow
already scoped.

AUDIT FIRST, before this addition: does any Time In/Out mechanism exist
anywhere in the platform today, even as a stub or partial feature? Report
findings honestly, same as the prior audit found the Attendance Dashboard
was sample data - confirm what's real versus what only appears to exist.

DESIGN REQUIREMENTS for the clock mechanism, propose your approach for
each before implementing:
1. Basic integrity constraints: a Talent cannot clock in twice without
   clocking out first, cannot clock out before clocking in, etc.
2. A missed clock-out (Talent clocked in, never clocked out) is an
   EXCEPTION requiring resolution - same pattern as "no approved revision
   = Admin exception, not a zero-hour invoice" - it does not silently
   resolve itself or get dropped from the period's data.
3. Clock events are captured as real, unambiguous absolute timestamps
   (not local-time strings), displayed back to each party in their own
   relevant timezone - consistent with how the 48-hour deadline handling
   already separates the canonical clock (America/New_York) from
   what's displayed.
4. Confirm whether this is a simple button-press action (Clock In /
   Clock Out) within the Talent's existing platform session, with no
   location/device restriction, or propose otherwise with reasoning.

This clock-derived timesheet then feeds the full Client-visibility,
Talent-submission, OnSpot-approval workflow already confirmed. Everything
else from the prior scoping stands unchanged: the talent_invoices
schema, the America/New_York-anchored 48-hour auto-send, the credit_memo
cascading-forward design, the deposit-coverage check, and the separate
monthly Client invoice assembly.

Report the audit findings (does any real clock mechanism exist today)
and your proposed design for all four points above before implementing
anything. This is the true foundation everything else in this feature
depends on - worth getting right before building on top of it.
