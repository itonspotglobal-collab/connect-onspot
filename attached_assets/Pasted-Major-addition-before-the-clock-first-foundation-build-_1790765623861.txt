Major addition before the clock-first foundation build proceeds — a
SECOND, parallel engagement mode exists alongside the tracked one. This
does not replace anything already approved; it's a distinct path a
Client chooses between.

TWO ENGAGEMENT MODES, Client-selectable:

1. TRACKED (everything already approved and scoped) - Clock In/Out,
   timesheet approval, invoice derived from approved attendance,
   Extended Hours/Deductions apply.

2. GUARANTEED/UNTRACKED (new) - the Talent works flexibly, no Clock In/
   Out required at all. The invoice for each period is the full agreed
   rate, always - no Extended Hours or Deductions math applies, since
   there's no attendance data to compute it from.

CONFIRMED: the guarantee is NOT fully unconditional. Approved leave is
fine and doesn't reduce pay. But total non-performance or abandonment by
the Talent is a real exception that voids the guarantee for that period.

REAL DESIGN PROBLEM THIS CREATES: with no clock data at all, there's no
automated way to detect non-performance in this mode. The only realistic
signal is the CLIENT noticing and reporting it. Propose a lightweight
Client-initiated non-performance/abandonment claim mechanism for
GUARANTEED-mode engagements specifically:
- Client flags a specific period as non-performance/abandonment, with a
  reason.
- OnSpot reviews the claim (similar review-and-judgment pattern already
  used for timesheet disputes and missed Clock-Out exceptions).
- If substantiated, that period's invoice is adjusted/withheld rather
  than auto-generating the full guaranteed amount - propose the exact
  mechanism (does this block the invoice draft from generating at all
  until resolved, similar to how a pending timesheet dispute already
  blocks generation in the tracked mode?).
- If not substantiated / no claim raised, the period's invoice generates
  as the full guaranteed amount automatically, same fixed payment dates
  (25th/5th America/New_York) as the tracked mode.

TWO STRUCTURAL DEFAULTS I'm proposing - confirm or push back if a
different design fits the codebase better:
1. This mode is selected at JOB POSTING level (a Client decides how they
   want to manage this specific role when they post it), not per
   individual hire under that posting. Confirm this is sensible, or
   propose contract-level selection instead if that fits better given
   how job postings and hiring_contracts currently relate.
2. Once a hiring_contract is signed under one mode, it's LOCKED for the
   life of that engagement - switching modes requires ending and
   re-signing a new contract, consistent with this build's non-
   retroactive principle. Confirm or propose otherwise.

EVERYTHING ELSE UNCHANGED: the entire clock-first foundation, timesheet
workflow, Client visibility/dispute, OnSpot approval, talent_invoices
schema, 48-hour auto-send, credit_memo mechanism, and deposit-coverage
check remain exactly as already approved - all of that applies to the
TRACKED mode specifically. The GUARANTEED mode is a separate,
simpler invoice-generation path that coexists alongside it.

Report your audit of how job postings and hiring_contracts currently
relate (to answer point 1), and your proposed schema/workflow for the
non-performance claim mechanism, before implementing anything. Do not
start building either mode until this full picture is confirmed - there
have been enough revisions to this feature that getting the complete
model right now matters more than moving fast.
