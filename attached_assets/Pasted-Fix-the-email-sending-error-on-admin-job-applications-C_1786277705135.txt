The important distinction is this: your site’s admin authentication and your email provider’s authentication are two different things.

If the 401 is from your own API because something like requireAdmin, requireAuth, or isAuthenticated is attached to the send-email route, Replit can temporarily remove it from only that endpoint.

But if Microsoft/Outlook, Resend, SendGrid, etc. is returning the 401, then the email provider still needs valid credentials even though your site has no admin login yet.
