Please improve the “Transformations” / testimonials section on the homepage.

Current issue:
The section content is good, but the cards look boring and flat. They do not visually tell the story of the client transformation. The section should feel more premium, emotional, and engaging, similar to the editorial/storytelling feel of Apple’s website.

Goal:
Keep the current background and content, but redesign the testimonial cards so each one clearly tells a transformation story:
Before → After → Result

IMPORTANT:
- Keep the existing section background.
- Keep the existing section heading:
  “Real change. Real results.”
- Keep the section label:
  “TRANSFORMATIONS”
- Keep the existing testimonial names, roles, quotes, and metrics.
- Do not rewrite the testimonial content unless needed for tiny labels.
- Do not redesign the whole homepage.
- Focus on making the cards more visually compelling.

==================================================

Instead of plain quote cards, make each card feel like a mini case study.

- What changed

Use a visual structure like:

Quote

→ “automated excellence”

This should visually feel like a transformation, not just a quote.
