# Fix Featured Job Card Text Contrast on `/find-work/jobs`

You are working inside the existing **OnSpot Global / connect-onspot** codebase.

Fix the text visibility issue on **Featured Job cards** in:

`client/src/pages/FindWorkAllJobs.tsx`

The current Featured Job card has a dark/indigo premium header, but its lower content/body area uses text colors intended for a dark background even though the body background is light.

This causes:

* job summary text to appear too light / almost invisible
* skill tags to have poor contrast
* `View details` text to look washed out
* body content to feel inconsistent with regular job cards

Do not redesign the Featured Job card.

Keep the Featured header visually distinct.

Only fix the body text/readability so it matches the normal job cards.

---

# Current Problem

The Featured Job body currently has conditional styles similar to:

```tsx
featured
  ? "text-amber-100/70"
  : "text-slate-600 dark:text-slate-300"
```

and skill tags like:

```tsx
featured
  ? "text-amber-200/80"
  : "text-slate-600 ..."
```

However, the Featured card body itself is light-colored.

This creates low contrast.

---

# Desired Behavior

For the **lower body area** of Featured Job cards, use the same text colors as regular job cards.

The header can remain premium/dark.

The body should visually match normal cards.

Conceptually:

```text
FEATURED HEADER
dark indigo / premium styling
white/light text
badges
salary
posted date

────────────────────

FEATURED BODY
light background
dark slate summary text
normal slate skill chips
normal secondary action button
```

