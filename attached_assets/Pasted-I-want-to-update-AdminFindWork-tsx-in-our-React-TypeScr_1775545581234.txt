For Applicants:
- The process is designed to identify strong-fit candidates who align with company values, show motivation, can deliver results, and have the skills needed for the role.
- Application stage:
  - Submit application through the ATS.
  - Complete required written responses such as compensation history/expectation, motivation for the role, career goals, manager assessment insights, and a core values alignment scenario.
  - Applications missing the required value-alignment question may be disqualified.
- Career History Form (CHF):
  - Qualified candidates may be asked to complete a Career History Form.
  - This includes salary history, past managers, achievements, failures, self-ratings, manager ratings, and reasons for leaving previous roles.
- Initial Screening Interview:
  - Candidates will be assessed using the “Gets It, Wants It, Capacity to Do It” model.
  - Focus areas include understanding of the role, motivation, capacity, and culture fit.
- Topgrading Interview:
  - A deeper chronological interview covering roles since college.
  - Questions may include objectives, accomplishments, challenges, feedback from managers, and reasons for leaving.
- Competency Interviews:
  - For some roles, especially manager-level or delivery-related roles, candidates may go through additional competency or client interviews.
- Reference Checking:
  - Candidates may be asked to help facilitate 2–3 reference calls.
- Hiring Decision:
  - Final evaluation considers performance record, competencies, values alignment, coachability, and compensation fit.

For TA Team:
- Preparation & Job Scorecard:
  - Create the Job Success Profile (JSP) with measurable outcomes, values, and required skills.
  - Develop an A-Player Scorecard with competencies, performance metrics, and culture fit indicators.
- Attracting A-Players:
  - Publish smart job ads using compelling and results-focused language.
  - Highlight company culture, growth opportunities, and impact.
  - Include self-screening challenge questions.
  - Post on careers page, LinkedIn, and relevant platforms.
- Written Pre-Screening:
  - Collect applications via ATS.
  - Require written responses including compensation, motivation, career goals, manager insights, and core values alignment scenario.
  - Disqualify applicants who skip mandatory value-alignment questions.
  - Invite qualified candidates to complete the CHF.
- Initial Screening:
  - Conduct screening interview using the “Gets It, Wants It, Capacity to Do It” framework.
  - Document findings in interview notes.
- Topgrading Interview:
  - Conduct a chronological deep-dive interview covering all roles since college.
  - Capture role objectives, successes/failures, team and manager feedback, and departure reasons.
  - One interviewer leads while another or an AI agent records notes.
- Competency Interviews:
  - Assess top competencies from the scorecard using scenario-based and behavioral questions.
  - Client interview is required for delivery-related roles.
- TORC Reference Checking:
  - Require 2–3 reference calls.
  - Validate candidate claims, integrity, performance, team fit, and rehire likelihood.
- Hiring Decision:
  - Use the Topgrading Decision Grid:
    - Performance Record
    - Competency Match
    - Values Alignment
    - Coachability
    - Compensation Fit
  - Disqualify candidates showing consistent blame behavior or lack of evidence.
- Post-Hire Coaching:
  - Share the Job Scorecard on Day 1.
  - Provide a 30-60-90 Day Plan.
  - Hold monthly check-ins and use interview insights for coaching alignment.
