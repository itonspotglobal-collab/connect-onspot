Improve the mobile view of the Investors page to create a clean, modern, professional, and visually balanced experience with a strong **visual hierarchy**.

Use the attached mobile screenshot as the reference for identifying the current layout problems.

Make the entire headline horizontally centered on mobile screens.

Preserve the exact text:

**White text:**
"The outsourcing industry is being replaced."

**Gold text:**
"We are not watching. We built the replacement."

Requirements:
- Apply `text-align: center` to the headline and both text groups on mobile.
- Center the headline container horizontally.
- Use responsive font sizing with `clamp()` to avoid oversized text.
- Maintain strong typography and readable line heights.
- Ensure natural, balanced line wrapping.
- Provide adequate breathing room between the white and gold portions.
- Maintain the existing font family and brand colors.
- Avoid awkward word breaks, clipping, and horizontal overflow.

The headline should be the main focal point when the user opens the page.
