`Talent proposed a new interview time`

`My Interviews`

# DESIRED NEGOTIATION MODEL

The interview scheduling/rescheduling flow must have clear ownership of the pending response.

Example:

Client proposes 7:00 AM
→ awaiting Talent

Talent proposes 8:00 AM instead
→ awaiting Client

Client accepts 8:00 AM
→ confirmed at 8:00 AM

OR:

Client proposes 9:00 AM instead
→ awaiting Talent again

Talent accepts 9:00 AM
→ confirmed at 9:00 AM

Do NOT auto-confirm a counter-proposal.

Both parties must agree on the exact same time.

# BOTH-SIDES AGREEMENT RULE

An interview is only `confirmed` when:

Party A proposes exact time X
→ Party B explicitly accepts exact time X.

A proposal alone is NEVER confirmation.

A counter-proposal invalidates the previous pending proposal for acceptance purposes, while preserving it in immutable history.

Do not delete old proposals.
