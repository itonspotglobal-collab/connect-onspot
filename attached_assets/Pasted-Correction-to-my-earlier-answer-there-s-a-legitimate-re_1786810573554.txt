Correction to my earlier answer — there's a legitimate reason hourly figures exist,
I was wrong to say retire the concept entirely. Here's the actual distinction:

The FLAT rate (Half-Day/Full-Time) is what Client agrees to pay and Talent expects
to receive. Nobody chooses "hourly billing" as an option — that part of my earlier
answer stands. BUT when actual attendance falls short in a billing period (Talent
absent 2 days of the month), the invoice needs to deduct for time not worked, and
that requires an hourly-equivalent figure derived from the flat rate.

This is invoicing math, not a rate anyone sets. It should be:
- Computed as (flat_rate ÷ standard_period_hours) at invoicing time
- Applied only to calculate attendance-based deductions on an Engagement's invoice
- Never exposed as a field Client or Talent enters directly
