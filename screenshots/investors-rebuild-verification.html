Font verification and provisional choice

Hyperwave One was absent from the live official Google Fonts catalog (1,950 families), and its exact Google Fonts CSS request returned HTTP 400. The owner subsequently authorized a provisional verified Google Fonts match rather than blocking the rebuild.

  • Permanent Marker — selected provisionally: heavy, mostly unconnected marker forms are closest to the reference. Uppercase and a slight slant give the strongest match. Mixed case naturally retains much of its uppercase-like appearance.
  • Kaushan Script: mixed case is flowing and connected; those loops depart from the reference's blockier uppercase brush lettering. Uppercase becomes more decorative.
  • Caveat Brush: loose painted forms, but narrower and lighter than the reference in both cases.
  • All three families were verified in the live catalog and actually loaded in the comparison browser. Rock Salt also exists, but its thinner, scratchier strokes were not selected.

Only “Without” uses the accent font. One accent-font token controls the provisional family, with font-display: swap and a cursive fallback. Exact Hyperwave One remains a future replacement requiring a web-licensed WOFF2 file; the provisional font is not claimed to be identical.
